\section{Model specification}
Let $y_i$ denote the satisfaction level of customer $i$. We assume that
$$
y_i\ |\ p_i\sim\ \textbf{Bernoulli}(p_i)
$$
where $p_i$ is the probability that customer $i$ is satisfied.
We model the probability $p_i$ as a function of the covariates $X$ as
$$
\text{logit}(p_i)=\ln\left(\cfrac{p_i}{1-p_i}\right) = \beta_0+x_i\beta + \beta_{\text{mid}}\cdot\texttt{midflight\_delay}_i .
    \label{eq:logit_model}
$$
where $\beta_0$ is the intercept in the logit space and $\beta$ is the vector of regression coefficients of the remaining original covariates $X$ (after skewness adjustment, eventual hot-encoding for categorical variables and data augmentation), $x_i$ is the covariates set of customer $i$.
The mid-flight delay is deliberately kept outside the main design matrix and
given its own coefficient $\beta_{\text{mid}}$, paired with an explicit
per-row sampling statement $\texttt{midflight\_delay}_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$ in the JAGS model.
